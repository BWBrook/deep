# Beyond the scalar hazard: what the same pairwise tables leave open in the full labelled class (Fable)

Files: `labelled_class_probe.py`, `labelled_class_probe.json`, `labelled_class_probe.log`. Run from the repo root:
`uv run --python 3.12 --with numpy --with scipy python ideation/b_screen/labelled_class_probe.py` (about 45 s).

## Question

Astra's asymmetric witness (`asymmetric_pairwise_witness.md`) shows by a continuity (coupling) argument that exact
exchangeability is not needed for the reversal. The substantive version of the construction objection is different:
the paper's class is the set of hit laws generated by one scalar regional hazard (conditionally iid given theta), a
strict subset of all joint hit laws with the same pairwise tables. Does widening to the full class change what
complete pairwise information identifies?

## Class and machinery

L(p,q): all laws Q on labelled hit vectors x in {0,1}^4 with P(x_i = 1) = p for every patch and P(x_i = x_j = 1) = q
for every pair, i.e. every complete pairwise hit table equal to the exchangeable one. A polytope of dimension
16 - 11 = 5; the scalar-hazard (p,q)-class is a 2-parameter curved subset of it. The chain is the labelled 16-state
occupancy chain (no count aggregation): occupied patch i dies with probability e0 + (e1 - e0) x_i (R_i halves the
excess at patch i), survivors recolonise empties independently with probability 1 - (1-c)^m (C doubles c),
all-occupied start, absorbing 0, T = 50. Validation: for the exchangeable laws Glo and Ghi as pattern laws the
labelled chain reproduces the certified Delta values -0.1205203070 and +0.0320927350 to all digits.

Two ranking objects: Delta_1 = P_{R_1} - P_C (buffer a fixed patch) and Delta* = max_i P_{R_i} - P_C (buffer the best
patch). Search: polytope vertices from 600 random-objective LPs, 1500 random convex combinations, Nelder-Mead in
null-space coordinates. Vertex witnesses are reconstructed exactly from their support and re-evaluated in exact
rational arithmetic. Every extreme is an attained value: a lower bound on the class range, never a class extremum.
The 16-case design of the n=4 certificates is used, with the certified scalar-hazard ranges for comparison.

## Result

| case (q, e0, e1, c) | scalar-hazard class, certified Delta range | full labelled class, witnessed Delta_1 range | identification |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3/100, 1/50, 9/10, 1/10 (reference) | [-0.12052, +0.03209], unidentified, regret 0.03209 | [-0.17925, +0.06434] (both exact vertices) | unidentified; regret lower bound 0.06434, twice the scalar-hazard value |
| 3/50, 1/50, 9/10, 1/10 | [+0.03101, +0.11044], R identified | [-0.05342, +0.11246] (both exact vertices) | **loses identification**; regret lower bound 0.05342 |
| other 14 cases | identified (12 C, 2 R) | ranges widen by 1-40% at each end, no sign change found | identification not contradicted by witnesses (not certified in the wider class) |

Delta* coincided with Delta_1 at every found extreme: the maxima coincide by relabelling symmetry of the class, and
the minimisers found are themselves symmetric.

## What the extremal laws are

All four extreme witnesses in the two highlighted cases are exchangeable (pattern probability depends only on the
hit count) but are NOT scalar-hazard mixtures. Reference case:

- Delta_1 minimiser: P(K=0) = 39/50, each single hit 1/100, each pair 3/100, no triple or quadruple hits. "At most
  two patches are ever hit in a step." Its third moment (a specific triple all hit) is 0, below the scalar-hazard
  lower bound q^2/p = 0.009. Strong negative higher-order dependence: a localised disturbance footprint.
- Delta_1 maximiser: P(K=0) = 69/100, each single hit 7/100, all four hit 3/100, nothing in between. "Either one
  patch or the whole region." Third moment 3/100 = q, above the scalar-hazard upper bound 0.02456.

These are finite exchangeable laws with no de Finetti representation: pairwise tables identical to the regional
hazard model's, but higher-order co-occurrence outside what any mixture of independent hits can produce. The
scalar-hazard model is therefore not merely a symmetry assumption; it is a positive-dependence-structure assumption
(hits extendable to an infinite exchangeable sequence) that excludes localised disturbances of bounded footprint
and all-or-one regimes.

## Consequences for the paper argument

1. The negative (information) claim strengthens monotonically with class size and is untouched.
2. The positive results are class-conditional: "15/16 identified" holds for the scalar-hazard class; in the full
   labelled class at least one of those cases is unidentified and the remaining 14 have no certificate. The
   unimodal-restoration theorem is a statement about a scalar hazard law G and has no analogue in the wider class.
3. The ecological framing should say explicitly that the model assumes a regional (footprint-unbounded) hazard, and
   that localised disturbances break that assumption in a way that widens, not narrows, the ambiguity.
4. Asymmetry per se did not matter in this probe: the search over asymmetric vertices found nothing beyond the
   symmetric non-mixture laws. Astra's coupling witness and this probe answer different questions; this one is the
   version of the construction objection with content.

## Not shown

Class extrema in L(p,q) (no upper bounds; a 5-dimensional polytope with a degree-2T objective); anything at other
parameters or n; ecological calibration of footprint-bounded disturbance laws.
